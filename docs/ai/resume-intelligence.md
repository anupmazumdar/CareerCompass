# Resume Intelligence & ATS Diagnostics

## 1. Overview
The resume intelligence module provides deterministic ATS scoring, keyword extraction, and structural diagnostics for student resumes.

## 2. ATS Scoring Factors
1. **Contact Information (20%)**: Verifiable email address, telephone number, and LinkedIn/GitHub links.
2. **Section Hierarchy (30%)**: Clear headers for Education, Experience, Technical Skills, and Projects.
3. **Keyword Density (30%)**: Technical skills and action verbs matching target role taxonomy.
4. **Formatting & Readability (20%)**: Absence of multi-column tables, excessive graphics, or non-parseable elements.
